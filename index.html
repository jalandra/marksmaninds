Welcome to Marksman Industries

Marksman Industries is a leading manufacturer of high-precision pipe bending tools, tube bending dies, SPMs, gauges, jigs, fixtures, press tools, sheet metal components, and CNC & VMC machining solutions. Established in 1991 in Faridabad, we've built our reputation through consistent delivery of superior quality engineering products and innovative tool design services.

Our team of experienced engineers and skilled technicians brings decades of expertise in industrial tooling manufacturing to every project. We're committed to providing customized solutions that optimize your manufacturing processes, improve efficiency, and enhance product quality while reducing production costs.

At Marksman Industries, we take pride in our commitment to excellence, precision engineering, and customer satisfaction. We continuously innovate our machine tool designs and manufacturing processes to exceed client expectations. Looking for a reliable partner for your next manufacturing project? Contact us today.

Our Manufacturing Solutions

Precision tooling and engineering solutions custom-designed for your industry needs

Pipe & Tube Bending Tools

We specialize in advanced tool design for pipe bending applications, offering a comprehensive range of machine tools and pipe/tube bending dies including Bend Dies, Clamp Dies, Pressure Dies, specialized Mandrel types, Wiper Dies, and Collets. Our expert engineers select premium materials for each application (e.g., hardened tool steel for high-strength dies, bronze/special alloys for precision mandrels/wipers). Our precision tooling and custom machine tools deliver superior benefits including flawless finishes, longer operational lifespan, reduced manufacturing costs, and prevention of common defects such as wrinkling, flattening, slippage, galling and surface scratching in pipe/tube bending processes.

Learn More

Special Purpose Machines (SPMs)

We design and manufacture custom-engineered Special Purpose Machines (SPMs) tailored to your specific production requirements. Our SPMs are designed to optimize manufacturing processes, reduce cycle times, increase productivity, and improve product quality while decreasing labor costs. From semi-automated to fully automated solutions, our SPMs incorporate advanced engineering principles and precision components to deliver reliable performance for your industrial applications.

Learn More

Jigs & Fixtures

Our precision-engineered jigs and fixtures are designed to improve manufacturing efficiency, ensure consistent quality, and enhance production accuracy. We develop custom solutions for workholding, positioning, guiding, and supporting components during manufacturing operations. Our jigs and fixtures are built to withstand industrial environments while maintaining precise tolerances, resulting in reduced setup time, minimized errors, and increased productivity for your manufacturing processes.

Learn More

Precision Gauges

We are renowned manufacturers of high-precision measuring gauges and inspection tools designed for quality control applications. Our custom-engineered gauges ensure accurate dimensional verification, component validation, and compliance with strict manufacturing tolerances. From go/no-go gauges to complex profile gauges, our measuring instruments help maintain quality standards across various manufacturing processes while reducing inspection time and improving reliability of your quality control operations.

Learn More

Sheet Metal Components

We specialize in manufacturing high-precision sheet metal components and assemblies using advanced fabrication techniques. Our capabilities include custom press tools, progressive dies, and specialized forming tools to produce complex sheet metal parts with consistent quality and tight tolerances. From prototyping to high-volume production, we deliver sheet metal solutions for automotive, aerospace, electronics, and industrial applications with a focus on dimensional accuracy and surface finish quality.

Learn More

CNC & VMC Machining Services

Our state-of-the-art CNC and VMC machining centers deliver precision-manufactured components with exceptional dimensional accuracy and surface finishes. We provide comprehensive machining services for complex parts in various materials including steel, aluminum, stainless steel, and specialty alloys. Our advanced machining capabilities, combined with rigorous quality control procedures, ensure consistent part quality, reliable performance, and cost-effective manufacturing solutions for your precision component requirements.
